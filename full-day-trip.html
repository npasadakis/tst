Full Day Sailing Trip

From sunrise to sunset on Dia Island

A Complete Day at Sea

Embark on the ultimate sailing adventure to the pristine Dia Island. Join fellow adventurers for a full day of sailing, swimming, exploring, and enjoying the spectacular Greek coastline from morning until after sunset.

About This Trip

Our most popular offering, the Full Day Sailing Trip takes you to the beautiful Dia Island for a complete day of adventure. Departing in the morning from Heraklion port, you'll sail across the crystal-clear waters of the Aegean Sea to reach this uninhabited island paradise.

Dia Island is a protected natural reserve located just north of Heraklion, offering stunning turquoise waters, secluded bays, and incredible marine life. You'll have plenty of time to explore, swim, snorkel, and relax on this full-day adventure.

As the day progresses, enjoy a delicious lunch on board followed by more swimming and water activities. As the sun begins to set, we'll find the perfect spot to watch the spectacular sunset while enjoying dinner and drinks before sailing back to Heraklion port.

Daily Itinerary

09:00

Departure from Heraklion Port

Meet at Heraklion port, board the sailing boat, and receive a safety briefing. Coffee and light refreshments available as we prepare to set sail.

10:00

Sailing to Dia Island

Enjoy the sailing experience as we make our way to Dia Island. Relax on deck, help with the sails, or simply take in the beautiful coastal views.

11:00

First Swimming Stop

Anchor at a beautiful bay for swimming and snorkeling. Explore the underwater world or try the paddleboard.

13:00

Lunch on Board

Enjoy a delicious Greek lunch with fresh local ingredients. Wine, beer, and soft drinks are served throughout.

14:30

Explore More of Dia Island

Visit additional spots around the island for more swimming, snorkeling, or simply relaxing in the sun.

17:30

Sunset Viewing

Find the perfect location to watch the spectacular Greek sunset with drinks in hand.

18:30

Dinner & Return Journey

Enjoy dinner on board as we begin our return journey to Heraklion, arriving back at port around 20:00.

  • What to Bring: Swimsuit, towel, sunscreen, hat, sunglasses, camera, light jacket for evening
  • Fitness Level: Suitable for all fitness levels
  • Age: Suitable for all ages (children must be supervised)

  • Weather: Trips may be rescheduled in case of adverse weather conditions
  • Seasickness: Medication available on board if needed
