FAQ

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2-3 days in advance, especially during peak season (June-September).

What if the weather is bad?

Safety is our priority. If conditions are unsuitable, we'll reschedule your trip or provide a full refund.

Do I need sailing experience?

No experience necessary! Our professional skipper handles everything while you relax and enjoy.
