Half Day Adventure to Dia Island

Perfect for travelers with limited time, our half day sailing trips offer all the magic of Dia Island in a shorter timeframe. Choose between a morning adventure or a romantic sunset cruise.

About This Trip

Our half day sailing trips are designed for those who want to experience the beauty of Dia Island but have limited time available. Whether you choose the morning or sunset option, you'll enjoy approximately 4-5 hours of sailing, swimming, and relaxation.

Dia Island, a protected nature reserve just north of Heraklion, offers pristine waters perfect for swimming and snorkeling. The island's secluded bays provide shelter from the wind and create ideal conditions for water activities.

Both trips include a meal (lunch for morning trip, dinner for sunset trip) and unlimited drinks. Snorkeling equipment and paddleboards are available for all guests to use.

Morning Trip Itinerary

09:00

Departure from Heraklion Port

Meet at the port, board the boat, and receive a safety briefing. Coffee and refreshments available as we set sail.

09:30

Sailing to Dia Island

Enjoy the morning breeze as we sail towards Dia Island. Perfect time for photos and taking in the coastal views.

10:30

Swimming & Snorkeling

Anchor at a beautiful bay for swimming and snorkeling. Try the paddleboard or simply relax on board.

12:00

Lunch on Board

Enjoy a delicious Greek lunch with wine, beer, and soft drinks while anchored in a peaceful bay.

13:00

Return to Heraklion

Sail back to Heraklion port, arriving around 13:30 with the rest of your day free.

Sunset Trip Itinerary

16:00

Departure from Heraklion Port

Board the boat in the cooler afternoon hours. Refreshments served as we prepare to sail.

16:30

Sailing to Dia Island

Enjoy the afternoon sail with perfect lighting for photography and relaxation on deck.

17:30

Swimming & Activities

Take a refreshing swim in the golden hour light. Snorkeling and paddleboarding available.

18:30

Sunset Viewing

Find the perfect spot to watch the spectacular Greek sunset with drinks in hand.

19:30

Dinner & Return

Enjoy dinner on board as we sail back to Heraklion, arriving around 20:30.
