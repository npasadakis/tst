Charter Your Own Sailing Boat

Experience the ultimate freedom and flexibility with a private sailing charter. Perfect for families, couples, or groups who want a personalized sailing adventure tailored to their preferences.

About This Trip

Our private sailing trips offer you the exclusive use of one of our beautiful sailing boats for one or more days. Create your own itinerary, set your own pace, and enjoy an intimate experience on the crystal-clear waters around Heraklion and Dia Island.

Whether you're celebrating a special occasion, planning a romantic getaway, or simply want to enjoy the sea with your closest friends and family, a private charter gives you the freedom to make the most of your time on the water.

Our experienced skipper will guide you to the best spots, hidden coves, and secret beaches that only locals know about. You'll have the flexibility to spend as much time as you want swimming, snorkeling, paddleboarding, or simply relaxing on deck.

Good to Know

  • Duration: Flexible - from one day to multiple days
  • Group Size: Up to 8 guests (depending on boat)
  • Departure: Heraklion Port
  • What to Bring: Swimsuit, towel, sunscreen, hat, sunglasses, camera
  • Booking: Advance booking recommended, especially for multi-day charters
  • Weather: Trips may be rescheduled in case of adverse weather conditions
